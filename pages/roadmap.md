# Research priorities

*Unknown Hands* documents manuscripts associated with female scribes in pre-modern Europe. The catalogue links scribal evidence with physical production, texts, institutions, and manuscript locations.

## Catalogue coverage

The [Data]({{ '/data/' | relative_url }}) page derives its summaries from the published catalogue exports. [Browse & Search]({{ '/explore-database/' | relative_url }}) provides the individual records, relationships, and export controls. Record counts describe coverage; they do not measure scholarly completeness.

## Evidence and attribution

- Examine named and anonymous scribal contributions with their source references.
- Record the basis and certainty of attribution, rather than treating all identifications as equivalent.
- Review manuscript links, folio ranges, language descriptions, and institutional associations.
- Distinguish unrecorded information from explicit negative observations.

## Research tools

- Relate quantitative comparisons to manuscript images and catalogue evidence.
- Document counting units, missing data, and uncertain dates or places.
- Maintain accessible navigation, readable tables, and downloadable results.
- Connect transcription and handwriting samples to reliable manuscript and folio boundaries.

## Preservation and collaboration

- Prepare citable deposits of data and code in research repositories.
- Develop teaching activities grounded in manuscript evidence.
- Support record corrections and contributions through the [participation form]({{ '/participate/' | relative_url }}).
- Establish institutional partnerships for source access, catalogue review, and shared research.
