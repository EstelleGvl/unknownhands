    short_biography: Dorothea von Hof was the daughter of the merchant and guild representative
      Heinrich Ehinger. At the age of 16, she married Jerg (Jörg) von Hof, who served
      as mayor of Constance from 1499. After subscribing to the church, she made a
      pilgrimage to Einsiedeln at the age of 19. There, she donated clothing and jewelry,
      but was not clothed herself . Von Hof was later close to the Dominican nuns
      of St. Gall and is mentioned several times as a benefactor in the convent chronicle
      of St. Catherine's Abbey . However, there is no evidence of how the benefactor
      was connected to the abbey. After 15 years of childless marriage, the couple
      drew up a joint will in 1489. She died on February 27, 1501, and was buried
      in St. Stephen's Cathedral.
