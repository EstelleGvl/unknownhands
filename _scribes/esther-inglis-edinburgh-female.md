    comments: Esther Inglis (Esther Anglois, Esther Kello) was born in 1571, one of
      three children of Nicholas Langlois and his wife Marie Prescot, who fled to
      London from France about 1569 as Hugenot refugees. They had settled in Edinburgh
      by 1574 and apparently appeared on the poor roll for the years 1578 - 1580.
      At about this time Langlois was appointed Master of the French School, which
      position he retained until his death in 1611. Esther married around 1596 Bartholomew
      Kello, a clerk in Holy Orders, however she retained an anglicized version of
      her maiden name throughout her career. She died in 1624.
