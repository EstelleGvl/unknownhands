    short_biography: Anna Schott (ca. 1450-after 1521), daughter of the Strasbourg
      mayor Peter Schott (1427-1504) and sister of the jurist, theologian, and humanist
      Peter Schott (1460-1490), can be traced in Strasbourg as a Dominican nun of
      the convents St. Agnes and St. Margaret and St. Agnes (from 1475). She was extensively
      educated. During the fire of the Strasbourg city library in 1870, a manuscript
      written by her around 1500 (.Historia sanctorum") was lost (Schlechter, Schott,
      p. 467). In Schwarzach 19, it is the second known Anna Schott manuscript; it
      was considered lost (Cowie/Cowie (see Lit.), p.753).
