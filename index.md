# Unknown Hands

*Unknown Hands* documents female scribes in Christian Europe before 1600 through manuscripts, catalogue records, and research tools.

The catalogue links named scribes and attributed anonymous hands with texts, institutions, and physical production. Archival research, codicology, palaeography, and quantitative analysis support the study of attribution, multilingualism, and collaboration.

**Research aims:**
  - To provide an open, searchable database of manuscripts copied by women.
  - To map and visualize women’s scribal activity across Western Europe.  
  - To provide resources for researchers, teachers, and the wider public.  

The project is in active development. Learn more about its scope and methods on the [About]({{ '/about/' | relative_url }}) page, view a summary of the current [Data]({{ '/data/' | relative_url }}), or consult the [Bibliography]({{ '/bibliography/' | relative_url }}) for key references on female scribes.
